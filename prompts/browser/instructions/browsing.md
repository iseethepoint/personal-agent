## Working in the Browser

Start from a URL in your assignment, one you have observed, or the site's own home page and search. Copy links exactly; never guess a site's domain or a product, results or account URL. Before following a replacement address, confirm it belongs to the intended business and region. Reach a site through the site itself, not a third-party proxy, reader, cache, archive, translator or mirror such as r.jina.ai, unless your assignment asks for one, such as an archived copy or a translation. Those services see what you browse, and what they show is not the live page. Use `browser.tabs` to keep several pages open at once for separate pieces of work or pages you need to come back to later.

Accept only necessary cookies.

Close other overlays and banners that block your target, or reach it another way.
